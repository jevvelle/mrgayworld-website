THE NEXT INTERNATIONAL EDITION

THE NEW ERA CONTINUES

Mr. Gay World 2026 will bring representatives from around the world together from November 22 to December 1, 2026. Throughout the international competition, delegates will participate in advocacy, media, cultural, community, sponsor, and competition activities leading to the Mr. Gay World 2026 Coronation.

NOV 22

International Delegate Arrival

Delegates arrive and check in for the start of competition week.

NOV 23

Media Launch, Official Sashing & Rehearsal

Delegates are introduced to the press and officially sashed to represent their countries.

NOV 24

Tourism & Cultural Activities

Delegates explore the host destination's culture and landmarks.

NOV 25

Official Candidate Shoot

Official portraits and campaign imagery for each delegate.

NOV 26

Advocacy Presentation & National Directors' Meeting

Delegates present the causes they represent, alongside a meeting of National Directors.

NOV 27

Community Service & Rehearsal

Delegates take part in community engagement activities and rehearse for coronation night.

NOV 28

Sponsor Activities, Social Night & Quiz Night

A full day connecting delegates with official sponsors and partners, followed by evening social events.

NOV 29

Rest Day

A scheduled day off ahead of final rehearsals and coronation.

NOV 30

Full Rehearsal

Full run-through of the coronation night program.

DEC 1

Mr. Gay World 2026 Coronation

Mr. Gay World 2026 is crowned before delegates, press, and a global audience.
