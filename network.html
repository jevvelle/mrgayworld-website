REPRESENT YOUR COUNTRY

Mr. Gay World welcomes gay men who are prepared to represent their country with purpose, professionalism, authenticity, and commitment.

In countries with an official Mr. Gay World National Director, candidates should apply through their recognized national organization or official national selection process.

What If My Country Does Not Have a National Director?

If your country currently has no official Mr. Gay World National Director, you may contact the international organization directly. Our team will review your inquiry and advise you regarding the available process for representation, eligibility, and participation.

What We Want Candidates to Understand

Becoming a Mr. Gay World candidate is not simply about entering an international pageant. You will be representing:

  • Your country
  • Your community
  • Your advocacy
  • Your National Director or national organization
  • The values and standards of Mr. Gay World

Candidates should be prepared for media activities, advocacy presentations, interviews, cultural exchange, competition activities, community engagement, and international representation.

OUR GLOBAL NETWORK

MANY COUNTRIES. ONE INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATION.

Mr. Gay World is built through the collaboration of National Directors, delegates, titleholders, partners, advocacy organizations, host destinations, and communities from around the world.

Every country brings something different to Mr. Gay World. Different cultures. Different LGBTQIA+ experiences. Different challenges. Different approaches to advocacy. Different stories.

The role of the international organization is not to erase those differences. It is to create a global platform where they can be represented.

GLOBAL FRANCHISE NETWORK

BRING MR. GAY WORLD TO YOUR COUNTRY

Mr. Gay World operates internationally through its network of official National Directors and franchise holders. A National Director is responsible for developing and representing the official Mr. Gay World organization within his or her licensed territory.

National Directors may organize a national competition or approved selection process, develop national advocacy initiatives, build partnerships, promote the Mr. Gay World brand, prepare their official delegate, and participate in the international organization.

Becoming a National Director is not an employment position. It is an official franchise and licensing opportunity within the Mr. Gay World international network.

National Director Responsibilities May Include

  • Developing the Mr. Gay World brand within the licensed country
  • Selecting the country's official international representative
  • Organizing national competitions or approved candidate-selection programs
  • Preparing delegates for the international competition
  • Developing advocacy and community programs
  • Securing local sponsors and partners
  • Coordinating media and promotional activities
  • Maintaining the standards and reputation of the international organization
  • Communicating regularly with Mr. Gay World International
  • Supporting the delegate throughout his international journey

HOST DESTINATION

HOST THE WORLD

Mr. Gay World welcomes proposals from destinations, tourism organizations, government agencies, producers, event organizations, franchise holders, and qualified partners interested in hosting an international edition of Mr. Gay World.

Hosting Mr. Gay World brings delegates, National Directors, international guests, media, partners, and LGBTQIA+ communities together in one destination.

A host country becomes more than the location of an international pageant. It becomes part of the history and global identity of Mr. Gay World.

Hosting Opportunities May Include

  • Official delegate accommodation
  • International arrival and transportation
  • Tourism and cultural activities
  • Advocacy events
  • Community engagement
  • Media activities
  • Sponsor activations
  • Preliminary competitions
  • Rehearsals
  • International coronation event

Mr. Gay World believes strong advocacy requires strong partnerships.

We welcome collaborations with LGBTQIA+ organizations, healthcare institutions, advocacy groups, tourism authorities, government organizations, media companies, global and local brands, community organizations, and businesses that share our commitment to representation, equality, health, inclusion, and responsible visibility.

Partnership with Mr. Gay World should mean more than placing a logo on an event. The strongest partnerships help create opportunities, educate communities, expand advocacy, connect people with resources, and create meaningful international engagement.
